About Us

Who we are and what NATUREConnect is about

Our Mission

NATUREConnect connects people with outdoor events and experiences. Our goal is to make discovering, booking and managing outdoor activities simple and transparent.

For Participants

Participants can explore events, choose options and manage their bookings in one place.

For Organizers

Organizers can publish events, manage capacities and handle bookings efficiently.

To become an organizer on NATUREConnect, please contact us. We verify organizer accounts before enabling organizer features to ensure quality and safety for participants.

Project Background

NATUREConnect is developed as part of an academic project focusing on event management, usability and clean system design.
