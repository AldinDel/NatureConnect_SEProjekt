How NATUREConnect handles your personal data

We take the protection of your personal data very seriously. This privacy policy explains how NATUREConnect collects, processes and uses personal data when you use our platform.

Personal data is processed exclusively for the purpose of providing and improving our services, handling bookings and ensuring platform security.

Data Sharing

Your data will not be shared with third parties unless required by law or necessary for the operation of the platform.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data at any time. If you have any questions regarding data protection, please contact us.
