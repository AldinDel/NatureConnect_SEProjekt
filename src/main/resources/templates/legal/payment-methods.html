Available payment options at NATUREConnect

Available Payment Methods

During checkout, you can choose one of the following payment methods: Invoice, on-site payment, credit card or PayPal.

Partial Payments

In some cases, payments can be recorded in multiple steps (for example, if a part is paid first and the remaining amount later). The invoice can show the paid amounts and the open balance.

Splitting Invoices

Individual items can be moved to a separate invoice. This allows multiple invoices where each invoice contains only part of the items.

Important

The available options may depend on the event and organizer. The final payment details are shown during checkout.
