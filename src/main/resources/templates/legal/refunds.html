Refund & Cancellation

Cancellation rules and refund conditions at NATUREConnect

Cancellation Deadlines & Fees

A booking can be cancelled up to an agreed cancellation deadline. Depending on the cancellation date, different costs may apply:

• No costs if cancelled up to four weeks before the event
• 25% of the booking price if cancelled up to two weeks before the event
• 70% of the booking price if cancelled up to three days before the event
• 100% of the booking price if cancelled at short notice (from three days before the event), unless otherwise agreed

Event Cancellation

If an event is cancelled, all participants who booked the event will be notified. If a deposit has already been paid, the organizer will refund it (handled via an external system).

Refund Processing Time

Refunds are typically processed within 5 business days after approval, depending on the payment method and external processing.
